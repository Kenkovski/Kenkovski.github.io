PrinceofAsia Uploader

PrinceofAsia Uploader is a personal desktop app (Windows) used by the creator PrinceofAsia to send the creator's own edited gaming videos from the creator's PC to the creator's own TikTok account as drafts. It is not offered to other users.

Features

Log in with TikTok

One-time sign-in with TikTok Login Kit (OAuth with PKCE). The app asks only for the permissions it needs.

Upload as draft

Sends a finished vertical MP4 to the owner's TikTok inbox using the Content Posting API (Upload to TikTok).

Owner stays in control

Nothing is posted automatically. The owner opens TikTok, adds the caption and decides whether to post.

How it works

  1. The account owner signs in with TikTok once and authorizes the app.
  2. The owner picks a finished video on the PC; the app uploads it to the owner's TikTok inbox as a draft.
  3. The owner reviews the draft in the TikTok app and posts it, or deletes it.

Permissions used

  • user.info.basic - to show which TikTok account is connected.
  • video.upload - to upload the owner's videos as drafts.

Data

Login tokens are kept only in a local file on the owner's computer and are used only with TikTok's official API. See the Privacy Policy and Terms of Service.
