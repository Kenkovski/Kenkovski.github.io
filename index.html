PrinceofAsia Uploader

PrinceofAsia Uploader is a small personal desktop tool used by the gaming creator PrinceofAsia to send the creator's own edited gaming videos (SMITE 2 gameplay clips) from the creator's PC to the creator's own TikTok account as drafts.

How it works

  1. The account owner logs in once with TikTok (Login Kit) and authorizes the app.
  2. The tool uploads a finished vertical video file to the owner's TikTok inbox as a draft (Content Posting API, Upload to TikTok).
  3. The owner opens the TikTok app, reviews the video, writes the caption and decides whether to post it.

The tool never posts anything publicly on its own and is not offered to other users.
