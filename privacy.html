Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 30, 2026

What data the app uses

When the owner logs in with TikTok, the app receives from TikTok:

  • basic profile information (open ID, display name, avatar), to confirm the correct account is connected;
  • an access token and refresh token, so the app can upload drafts to that account.

Where it is stored

These values are stored only in a local file on the owner's own computer. They are not sent to any server other than TikTok's official API, and they are not sold, shared or used for advertising or analytics.

Videos

The app uploads video files chosen by the owner directly to TikTok. No copies are sent anywhere else.

Other people's data

The app does not collect any data about other TikTok users or viewers.

Deleting data

Access can be removed at any time in the TikTok app (Settings and privacy > Security > Apps and services permissions). The owner can delete the locally stored token file at any time.
